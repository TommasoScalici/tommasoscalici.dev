## Disclaimer

**This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of any government agency.**
It is a third-party tool designed to calculate the Italian Tax Code (Codice Fiscale) using the standard public algorithm.

There is no guarantee of accuracy. The personal data entered by the user (first name, last name, date of birth, place of birth, gender) are used solely for calculation purposes. The app does not replace professional advice and does not guarantee the legal validity of the generated code.

## Privacy Policy

<p class="text-sm text-muted">Last updated: 2026-01-03</p>

### Data Controller

The data controller for the personal data collected through the app is **Tommaso Scalici**, the author of the app.

### Types of Personal Data Collected

The app itself does not collect personal data from the user for tracking or marketing purposes.

- **Google Account Authentication:** Access via Google account implies acceptance of these terms and is used strictly to collect a unique, non-traceable ID. This ID allows for the storage and synchronization of saved contacts across your devices.
- **Calculation Data:** Information entered for tax code calculation and generated "cards" are stored locally on your device and in your personal Google Cloud account storage (which is necessary for synchronization).
- **External Service:** The tax code calculation relies on an external service, <a href="https://www.miocodicefiscale.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">miocodicefiscale.com</a>. Please refer to their <a href="https://www.miocodicefiscale.com/en/privacy-policy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Privacy Policy</a>.
- **Document Scanning:** Health card/ID scanning uses Google's Vertex API. Refer to the <a href="https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/docs/shared-responsibility" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Cloud AI Privacy Policy</a>.

### Legal Basis for Processing

The processing of personal data is based on the user's consent, which is automatically granted by accessing and using the app.

### Data Sharing

The user's personal data and stored tax codes are **never shared with third parties** by the developer. Data is shared only with the aforementioned essential technical service providers (Google, miocodicefiscale.com) strictly to enable app functionality.

### Data Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the app service. Users can delete their data at any time via the "Delete Account" feature in the app.

### Security

The data controller adopts appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

### Changes to this Policy

This privacy policy may be modified at any time. Users are encouraged to periodically review this page for the latest information.
