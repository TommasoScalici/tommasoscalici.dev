---
title: 'Tax Code - Delete Account'
description: 'Instructions on how to delete your account and data from the Tax Code app.'
---

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from the **Tax Code** app, please follow these simple steps:

1. Open the **Tax Code** app on your device.
2. Tap on your **Profile Icon** located in the top-right corner of the app bar.
3. Select the **"Delete Account"** option from the menu.
4. Confirm your action.

This action will **permanently delete** your account and all associated data from our servers. This process is irreversible.

<div class="not-prose mt-8 rounded-lg border border-yellow-500/20 bg-yellow-500/10 p-4 text-sm text-yellow-200">
    <strong>Note:</strong> If you only wish to delete your saved cards (local data) without deleting your account, you can simply delete the cards individually within the app. No other data is stored on our servers.
</div>
